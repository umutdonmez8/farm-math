Farm Math: Count & Play — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Farm Math: Count & Play (Turkish name: Çiftlikte Sayıyorum) is a math game for children ages 5–7. This policy applies to both the iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play) versions. Protecting children's privacy is a core principle of the app.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data. The app:

  • does not ask for an account or sign-in, and does not ask for or store names, email addresses, location, photos or audio;
  • shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or other third-party SDKs;
  • connects only to the store (App Store or Google Play) to make a purchase, and sends no personal data off the device;
  • does not track users.

What is stored on the device

Stars earned in activities, farm items that have been unlocked and settings (spoken instructions, music, sound effects, language) are stored only on the device. This information never reaches the developer or anyone else. It can be removed completely with "Reset progress" in the parent area, or by deleting the app.

Purchases

The Grade 1 content ("All of Grade 1") is purchased through Apple's App Store on iPhone and through Google Play on Android. The developer never sees your payment details; the app only learns from the store whether the purchase was made. The purchase screen is behind a parental gate that children can't pass.

Children

The app is designed to process no personal data, in line with Apple's Kids Category guidelines, the Google Play Families Policy and the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and will apply with the next version of the app.
